Privacy Policy

Nostelune: Restore Old Photos

Last Updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nostelune: Restore Old Photos ("Nostelune," "the App," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application to restore, enhance, colorize, or otherwise process photographs.

We aim to process only the information reasonably necessary to provide the App's features, process restoration requests, manage subscriptions, maintain security, and respond to support requests.

Important privacy information: AI photo restoration requires online processing through third-party services. When you choose to restore a photo, the selected image is transmitted to our processing infrastructure and AI processing provider. Please do not submit images you are not authorized to use or that you do not wish to share with these services.

1. Information We Process

Photos and images. When you select a photograph for restoration, we process the image and the information necessary to complete your request. Depending on the feature you choose, this may include the original image, an intermediate image, and the resulting restored image.

Photographs may contain personal information, such as faces, family relationships, locations, or other details visible in the image. You do not need to provide a person's name or other identity information to use the restoration features.

Technical and request information. Our service infrastructure and third-party providers may process technical information associated with a request, such as IP address, request time, device or network information, error details, and processing status, as necessary for service delivery, security, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention.

Subscription and purchase information. If you purchase a subscription, purchase-related information may be processed to verify transactions, determine subscription status, provide paid features, and support purchase restoration. This may include transaction records, product identifiers, subscription status, and an app-specific customer identifier.

Payments for App Store subscriptions are handled through Apple's payment systems. We do not receive your complete payment card number through RevenueCat.

Support communications. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, including your email address and the contents of your request.

2. How We Use Information

We use information as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide AI-powered photo restoration and image enhancement.
  • Receive images, submit processing requests, and return results.
  • Display restoration results and support saving images to your device.
  • Verify subscriptions and enable the features included in your plan.
  • Diagnose technical errors, maintain service reliability, and prevent abuse.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use photographs submitted for restoration to advertise unrelated products or services. We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration or share photographs for third-party advertising purposes.

3. Photo Processing and Third-Party Services

Nostelune uses third-party infrastructure and AI services to perform online photo processing. Depending on the feature and request, these services may include:

Cloudflare Workers. Our Cloudflare Worker acts as the backend intermediary between the App and the AI processing service. It receives restoration requests, communicates with the processing provider, and helps return processing results.

Replicate. We use Replicate-hosted AI models to perform image restoration and related processing. Images and model inputs may be transmitted to Replicate for inference. Replicate may process or retain inputs, outputs, and technical request information according to its applicable terms, privacy policy, and service configuration.

RevenueCat. We use RevenueCat to support subscription management, purchase verification, and entitlement status. RevenueCat may process transaction information, app-specific customer identifiers, and technical information required to provide its services.

Apple. Apple provides App Store distribution, in-app purchase infrastructure, and iOS photo-library permission controls. Apple's own privacy policy and applicable service terms govern its processing of information.

These providers process information as necessary to deliver their services, maintain security, meet legal requirements, or fulfill other purposes described in their applicable policies. Their own data handling practices and retention periods may apply.

4. Photo Library Access and Local Storage

The App may request access to selected photos or permission to save restored images to your photo library. You can manage these permissions through iOS settings.

Saving a restored image to your photo library is initiated by you. Images saved to Apple's Photos library are subject to your device settings and Apple's platform behavior.

The App may retain image files or restoration records locally to support its features. Local copies, cached files, and history records may remain on your device until removed by the App, deleted by you, or removed through applicable device or app management processes.

Deleting a local record does not necessarily delete a copy previously transmitted to a third-party processing provider. Such copies, if any, are governed by the provider's retention practices and applicable requirements.
